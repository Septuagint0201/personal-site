01

A fragile tether

Click to send a small impulse. Near its starting place, the crystal finds its way home. Pull it beyond the blue halo and the tether breaks.

02

Something softer

The released glass softens, keeping the memory of its three-sided shape. Drag its surface to stretch or press it into the ground. Right-drag downward, or hold Squeeze, to flatten it. The further you pull, the more it resists. Let go and it gently finds its shape again. Loose grains can rest on the glass, then slide away. If it comes to rest beyond the frame, the desert drops it back from above.

03

A surface with memory

Trace a groove and watch loose grains skip across the sand. Lift and retrace to cut into the next layer. The glass pushes ridges out around its impressions; steep edges settle and compressed tracks look smoother. Smooth sand clears only the ground. Start again begins a new experiment.

The camera stays fixed. P pushes, Space pauses, and R starts again. Interacting resumes the study. On touch screens, drag with one finger and use Hold to squeeze after release.

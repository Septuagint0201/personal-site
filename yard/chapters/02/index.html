FIELD NOTES / CHAPTER 02

A room without
weight.

Liquid gathers at the bends of a mirrored framework. It lets go, drifts, joins another drop, and finds its way back into the metal. A closed current, with no up or down.

01

Step insideClick the room to capture the mouse. Move it freely to look; Esc releases your cursor. Use W A S D to walk. On touch screens, use the left joystick and drag with your other thumb. Arrow keys also turn the view.

02

Touch the currentLeft click to Pulse, right click to Split, and hold E to Gather. The reticle gently links to nearby glass within reach. Action buttons remain available; P and F are keyboard alternatives. Every gesture preserves the amount of glass.

03

Follow the lightStrip lights trace long reflections across chrome and bend through moving glass. Reflection, refraction, dispersion and absorption are evaluated along light paths in real time. Ultra gives those paths more detail; High keeps a sharp image on smaller screens.

There are three hidden responses in the room. Stay curious. Sound is off until you choose it. Space pauses the flow; R returns to the entrance. Reduced-motion preferences start with the flow paused.

Small gestures leave a trace. Discover a response here, then revisit it whenever you like.

Your discoveries stay in this browser. The room remembers only these three discoveries.

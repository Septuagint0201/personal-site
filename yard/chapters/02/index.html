A FEW FIELD NOTES

A path through
clear matter.

Light rays meet a moving glass surface, bend as they enter, travel through its thickness, and bend again as they leave. Red, green and blue take slightly different paths.

01

Three optical sculpturesChrysalis folds a solid crystal around an open heart. Nave passes through liquid gothic vaults. Undertow layers three fluted curtains above a rippling basin.

02

Traced in real timeA WebGL shader finds curved surfaces and follows red, green and blue along separate paths through multiple glass boundaries. Fresnel reflection, total internal reflection, and absorption through the glass create its polished depth. Reflected rays sample the architectural room; floor caustics are an artistic light field.
